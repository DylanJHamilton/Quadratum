PROJECT ULTIMA — Complete Quadratum V1 Bug Tracker
Here are all 37 tracked issues collected from ARC//FORM, Folio & Field, Morrow, Fairway Motor Co., ORVAINE, VANTIER, KINVAR, and our direct testing observations.
Important distinction: These are 37 tracking entries, not 37 confirmed unresolved bugs. Some have already been fixed on individual demo branches, some overlap, and others are optional feature enhancements. Their resolution in main still needs verification.
