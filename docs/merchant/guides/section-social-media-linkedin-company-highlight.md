# Social - LI highlight

[All documentation](../README.md) · [Support](../SUPPORT.md)

Media and interaction.

## Add and configure

1. Open Online Store > Themes > Customize for an unpublished copy. Choose the page template, select Add section, and choose Social - LI highlight.
2. Connect your own content and media before adjusting layout, typography, color, and spacing. Section overrides take priority where the section offers them.
3. Preview desktop and mobile, test any links or controls, and save. Publish only after reviewing the complete customer journey.

Manual company content and statistics only. Verify your claims and configure the actual destination; the theme does not connect to LinkedIn or retrieve counts.
