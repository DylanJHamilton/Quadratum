## Add and configure

1. Open Online Store > Themes > Customize for an unpublished copy. Choose the page template, select Add section, and choose Account reset password.
2. Connect your own content and media before adjusting layout, typography, color, and spacing. Section overrides take priority where the section offers them.
3. Preview desktop and mobile, test any links or controls, and save. Publish only after reviewing the complete customer journey.

Legacy customer account template. Shopify owns account authentication, email delivery and session rules. Current customer accounts operate outside the theme; this section does not migrate accounts or add social login.
