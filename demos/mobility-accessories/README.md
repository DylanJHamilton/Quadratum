# OpenPath Living — Quadratum sample store

**Vertical:** Mobility & independent-living equipment  
**Branch:** `demo/sample-mobility-accessories-20261007`  
**Preview:** [Open the storefront source](./index.html)

Respectful, autonomy-first merchandising; high-contrast teal and slate, legible type, clear specs, specialist help, and no pity-led imagery.

## Storefront included

Responsive concept page with branded navigation, hero, shop filters, six sample products, collection story, newsletter sign-up, and a sample-bag interaction. It is a design prototype, not an installed Shopify theme or live commerce store. Preview buttons do not submit transactions or collect email.

## Collections and pages

Collections: Walkers, Ramps, Daily Living, Seating. Pages: Fit & Measurement Guide, Delivery & Setup, Returns, Accessibility Statement, Contact a Product Specialist. Show dimensions, capacity, compatibility, setup, delivery, and returns clearly.

## Asset checklist

Hero with disabled people in everyday settings, developed collaboratively and photographed with consent; product images with scale and dimensions; transfer/fit diagrams; packaging and setup details; meaningful alt text for every image.

## Product seed

[`catalog-seed.csv`](./catalog-seed.csv) lists the sample products shown on the page. It is a planning/import-mapping aid, not Shopify's native product-import format. Prices and product claims are placeholders.

## Before a Shopify draft launch

1. Replace sample copy, artwork, and prices with approved brand assets and verified product data.
2. Confirm manufacturer specifications, capacity limits, installation constraints, warranty, shipping/returns, and any funding details.
3. Add the pages above; configure navigation, inventory, tax, shipping, payment, and contact details.
4. Review accessibility, mobile layouts, product details, and purchase/lead forms in a Shopify draft.
