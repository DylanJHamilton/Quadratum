# Lead inquiry

[All documentation](../README.md) · [Support](../SUPPORT.md)

Forms and support.

## Add and configure

1. Open Online Store > Themes > Customize for an unpublished copy. Choose the page template, select Add section, and choose Lead inquiry.
2. Choose the card or stacked form layout and configure the visible fields. An email address is required for Shopify delivery.
3. Requests and custom fields are sent through Shopify contact forms. This section does not connect to a CRM.
4. Select Add block inside this section. Use the supported block types listed below, then drag them into the desired reading order.
5. Connect your own content and media before adjusting layout, typography, color, and spacing. Section overrides take priority where the section offers them.
6. Preview desktop and mobile, test any links or controls, and save. Publish only after reviewing the complete customer journey.

Uses Shopify’s native contact form and spam protection. Requests go to the store’s configured contact email. Custom fields are included in the inquiry; no CRM connection is required.
