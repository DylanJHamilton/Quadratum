# Footer

[All documentation](../README.md) · [Support](../SUPPORT.md)

Theme Settings.

## Add and configure

1. Open Online Store > Themes > Customize, then select Theme Settings and Footer.
2. Set shared footer defaults, then add the actual menu and content blocks inside the active footer section. Column count controls layout; it does not create menu items.
3. Use your own policies and contact information. Review narrow screens and retain the native Shopify credit in the submitted demo.
4. Set shared defaults here. A section with its own override may retain that override; select the section to adjust it.
5. Preview a home, product, collection, and cart page before saving shared changes.

## Footer layout choice

Choose and arrange footer designs in the Footer section group in the editor sidebar.

## Footer one — content

### Footer link columns

Drag the control to adjust footer link columns. Use only the range and increments shown below. Preview at mobile and desktop widths after changing spacing or dimensions.

Used by Footer One. Other footer designs use their own section controls.

Default: `4`.

Range: 1 to 6 ; step 1.

<details><summary>Reference for connected builders</summary>

Setting: `footer_columns`. Type: `range`.

</details>

## Footer one — announcement bar

### Enable footer announcement bar

Turn on “Enable footer announcement bar” to apply that behavior. Turn it off to remove that behavior. Other settings can affect whether the result is visible; check the section preview after changing it.

Used by Footer One. Other footer designs use their own section controls.

Default: `False`.

<details><summary>Reference for connected builders</summary>

Setting: `footer_announce_enable`. Type: `checkbox`.

</details>

### Footer announcement text

Enter the footer announcement text. Replace sample wording with your own content; leave optional fields empty when you do not need them.

Used by Footer One. Other footer designs use their own section controls.

Default: `Subscribe & save 10% on your first order.`.

<details><summary>Reference for connected builders</summary>

Setting: `footer_announce_text`. Type: `text`.

</details>
