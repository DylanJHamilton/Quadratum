# Content accordion

[All documentation](../README.md) · [Support](../SUPPORT.md)

Content and merchandising.

## Add and configure

1. Open a compatible section or parent block in the theme editor, select Add block, and choose Content accordion. The parent controls its available space and overall layout.
2. Configure the block’s content first, then its presentation. Drag the block in its parent to change its position.
3. Preview desktop and mobile, test any links or controls, and save. Publish only after reviewing the complete customer journey.

Compatible sections: Block section, Product block section.

## Behavior

### Allow multiple items open

Turn on “Allow multiple items open” to apply that behavior. Turn it off to remove that behavior. Other settings can affect whether the result is visible; check the section preview after changing it.

Default: `False`.

</details>
