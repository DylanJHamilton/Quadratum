# 3P - marketing funnel

[All documentation](../README.md) · [Support](../SUPPORT.md)

Content and merchandising.

## Add and configure

1. Open Online Store > Themes > Customize for an unpublished copy. Choose the page template, select Add section, and choose 3P - marketing funnel.
2. Connect your own content and media before adjusting layout, typography, color, and spacing. Section overrides take priority where the section offers them.
3. Preview desktop and mobile, test any links or controls, and save. Publish only after reviewing the complete customer journey.

Native links only. Set your actual destinations; blank links are omitted. Configure funnel forms, tracking and automation with the destination service. Replace example content with your actual offering.
